How the powder score works

Each day scores from 0 to 100. Snowfall does most of the work and tops out at 12″ in a day. Cold air adds points, because colder snow is lighter. Calm wind adds a little. Multi-day windows combine the daily scores, so two 6″ days beat one 6″ day but not one 12″ day.

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo at about 70% of the way up each mountain. When either model expects 2″ or more, a badge compares the main forecast with ECMWF: agree, differ, or split. Model check compares the last three days of modeled snow to snow depth gains measured at the nearest SNOTEL station (US only), so you know how far to trust the numbers.
